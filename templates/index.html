    Drill Down Project

    Welcome

    Welcome to Drill Down Project

    An AI-powered diagram studio for turning ideas into professional visuals — ERDs, sequence diagrams, use cases, flowcharts, swim lanes, and more — on an interactive canvas you can edit, connect, and export.

    • Multi-agent pipeline from brief to reviewed diagram
    • Full manual toolbox when you want precise control
    • Project save, transfer, and client-ready export APIs

    Getting started

    How it works

    Two paths, one canvas. Use AI when you want speed, or build by hand when you want full authorship.

    • AI path: open the AI panel, describe what you need, refine the brief, then Generate Now
    • Manual path: pick a diagram type, place shapes from the toolbox, and draw connectors
    • Drag, pan, rename, export SVG/JSON, and save projects anytime

    Agent 0 · Requirements Analyst

    How briefs are created

    Agent 0 chats with you first. When your prompt is sparse, it asks clarifying questions — actors, scope, constraints — until the generation brief is solid enough to build from.

    • Chat naturally; Send keeps the conversation going
    • When ready, use View Brief to inspect or edit the brief
    • Or click Proceed to skip questions and enhance with safe assumptions

    Generator agent

    How diagrams are generated

    The main Generator agent turns your approved brief into a structured diagram — nodes, relationships, layout — drawn live on the canvas.

    • Press Generate Now once the brief is ready
    • Shapes land as editable SVG elements — move, relabel, reconnect
    • Follow-up chat does not redraw until you generate again (by design)

    QC Auditor

    How quality control works

    After generation, the QC agent reviews structure and clarity for the diagram type — then surfaces optional suggestions you can act on manually.

    • Looks for missing entities, weak relationships, and labeling gaps
    • Shows a compact QC suggestions list when improvements are useful
    • Open the Log anytime to inspect Analyst → Generator → QC thinking
